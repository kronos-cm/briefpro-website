How It Works

Three Steps. One Responsibility Chain.

No autopilot. No black‑box output. Every transition is visible, every change traceable.

01

Record

Speak as you would on rounds. BriefPro transcribes in German, optionally with speaker separation. Audio is discarded after transcription.

  • Voice‑first, no template learning
  • Patient + Encounter context
  • Audio not persistent
02

Draft

12‑section standard with dynamic templates. AI suggests, marks missing data as [?] instead of inventing.

  • Pediatrics / Adults automatic
  • Diff‑based review per section
  • Accept · Reject · Edit
03

Approve

Status flow Resident → Attending → Final. Only approved content leaves the workflow. Complete audit trail.

  • Role‑based Approval‑Gates
  • Who · what · when per section
  • Export PDF / HL7 / Copy

FAQ

What procurement, IT and specialists ask.

Does BriefPro replace medical decisions?

No. BriefPro is documentation support. It makes no autonomous diagnostic or therapeutic decisions. Medical responsibility and final approval always remain with the clinical team.

Does BriefPro automatically write back to other systems?

No, not autonomously. Exports are triggered explicitly. Pilots typically start export‑first; HL7 / FHIR / KIS connectors follow in phases.

How fast can a pilot start?

Typically within a few days, without deep KIS integration. One department, clear success criteria, 4–8 weeks duration.

What data leaves the system?

Audio is discarded after transcription (standard). Transcripts and letters encrypted in the EU. No sharing with third parties without explicit consent.

Pediatrics support?

Yes. Pediatric intake with required fields (percentiles, vaccination status), age‑appropriate templates, guardrails against incomplete letters.

What does the pilot cost?

Pilot pricing is finalized individually based on clinic size, department scope, and integration needs. Expect no hidden games — we are transparent in the discovery discussion.
